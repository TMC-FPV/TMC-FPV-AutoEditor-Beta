# TMC-FPV Auto Editor

A simple, experimental video editor designed for FPV pilots.

TMC-FPV Auto Editor takes your DVR footage and automatically builds a video edit, helping turn long flights into watchable videos without spending hours manually cutting footage.

## 🚧 Community Beta

This is an experimental community beta. Features are actively being developed and tested, and results may vary between builds.

The software is developed by TMC-FPV and is provided for testing and feedback.

## Features

- Automatic video editing
- Random and chronological editing modes
- Music-aware editing
- Beat, bar and phrase-based cut selection
- Musical structure and transition awareness
- Different editing styles and presets
- Designed with FPV footage in mind

Features may change between releases.
